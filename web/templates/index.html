Mild Steel Metallurgy & UTM Testing Context

Physical metallurgy of structural mild steel welds, heat-affected zone grain growth, and testing at Vasavi College of Engineering.

Experimental UTM Testing

Conducted on the 600 kN Universal Testing Machine at Vasavi College of Engineering (Civil Engineering Department). Flat rectangular tensile bars (30 mm × 3 mm, 90 mm² area) were tested in unwelded base metal and welded conditions.

Weld Ductility Degradation

The tests reveal a drastic reduction in plastic elongation due to welding thermal cycles: unwelded mild steel achieves 28% - 31% elongation, whereas welded coupons drop to 13.9% - 22.5% (a 27% - 55% loss in ductility), with Specimen 7 failing directly in the weld zone.

AI Qualification & Safety

Our framework pairs Stacking Ensembles and PINNs with 50-pass Monte Carlo Dropout to establish conservative Minimum Guaranteed Strength (μ - 2σ) ratings and joint efficiency margins without requiring destructive coupon testing.
